Why this is a sidecar and not an in-file header
-----------------------------------------------
FASTA has no comment syntax that modern parsers accept. A '#' or ';' preamble is
read as sequence or raises an error in Biopython, samtools, HMMER and BLAST.
The file is therefore held byte-verbatim and its provenance recorded here.

Unlike defensins.fasta, this file is NOT a reconstruction. It existed on disk
throughout and was copied unchanged.

Known limitation inherited from upstream
-----------------------------------------
These peptides were predicted from UNSPLICED genomic sequence. The GTF used for
extraction (results/novel_genes/novel_genes.gtf) contains only transcript records
and no exon records, so `bedtools getfasta -split` had no blocks to split on and
returned whole genomic intervals with introns included.

47 of the 50 input sequences are multi-exon, so most ORFs here may read through
intron sequence. Two worked examples:

  MSTRG.31255.1  single-exon  -- UNAFFECTED. 104 aa ORF is clean.
  MSTRG.45757.2  4 exons      -- extracted interval 2,937 bp, of which 2,231 bp
                                 (76.0%) is intron. The 197 aa ORF spans local
                                 positions 30-623, of which 252 bp (42.4%) falls
                                 inside introns 2 and 3. The true spliced mRNA is
                                 only 706 bp.

Verify exon structure per candidate against
results/assembled/merged_assembly.gtf (which retains exon records) before relying
on any individual peptide.
